Direction A

Ish stoli  /  the worktable

The page is a workbench, not a brochure. The hero's main visual is a tray of real product photos running off both edges, with one empty slot cut into it: the seller's own photo is the missing piece. Below the fold the studio is a fixed two-pane workbench: controls on the left, the seller's product pinned on the right so it is never off screen. Annotation marks do real work, not decoration.
